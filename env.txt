# --- Per-draw cost (measured in this port's own comment) ---
# A written native storage binding is currently marked GPU-written WHOLE -- up to 8 MiB, including
# "data the program never touched" (buffer_cache.h:875-879). Sizing it to the word after the
# pointer is the port's own measured win: 30 ms a frame -> 21 with every binding sized. This is the
# opposite side of Fritchii's v5.6 fix: they removed a 64 KiB CAP that left writes untracked; our
# tree has no cap and over-marks instead, so it carries the cost without the bug.
EDEN_NX2_SSBO_WRITES_SIZED=1
